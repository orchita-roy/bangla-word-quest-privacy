# Privacy Policy for Bangla Word Quest

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Bangla Word Quest respects your privacy.

## Information Collection

Bangla Word Quest does not collect, transmit, sell, or share users' personal information.

The game does not require users to create an account or sign in.

## Local Game Data

Game progress, scores, coins, unlocked levels, and related gameplay information may be stored locally on the user's device. This information is used only to save game progress and is not transmitted to the developer or stored on an external server.

## Advertising

Bangla Word Quest does not display third-party advertisements.

## Third-Party Services

Bangla Word Quest does not use Firebase or other third-party services to collect users' personal information.

## Children's Privacy

Bangla Word Quest does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page.

## Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Bangla Word Quest Google Play Store listing.
